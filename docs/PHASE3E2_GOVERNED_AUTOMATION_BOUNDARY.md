# Phase 3E.2 Governed Automation Boundary Closure

Status: **closed at the governed automation boundary; full automation generalization not accepted**

Phase 3E.2 preserved the original S-K fresh-source acceptance result as `FAIL`. Later work did not reinterpret that result: S-L2 completed bounded scope, duplicate, review-reconciliation, and prospective scoreability repairs; S-L3 failed its adversarial duplicate-precision gate; S-L4 closed that duplicate-precision defect; and the S-L5 development experiment exposed primary-extraction truncation.

S-L6 then proved deterministic pre-call extraction partitioning independently of the semantic automation target. Under `PHASE3E2SL6_PRECALL_PARTITION_V1`, frozen-acceptance `forbid` mode uses `min(configured_max_chunk_chars, 10000)`, freezes the complete piece plan before the first call, and forbids response-driven splitting. The long development source completed with three planned pieces, zero output truncations or semantic retries, exact ordered Source reconstruction, and no cross-piece content, provenance, or merge loss.

S-L7 found that the remaining limitation was review precision rather than core correctness: among 121 propositions, 30 REVIEW decisions were legitimate and 27 were avoidable; 24 of the avoidable decisions belonged to the dominant atomicity false-positive family. Review burden was therefore source-dependent and high on this complex clean source.

The single preregistered S-L8 atomicity attempt passed the same-source feasibility census but released an existing legitimate negative control: adjacent `status` and `capability` propositions can remain independently updateable even when their structural fields otherwise align. The semantic implementation was reverted without tuning. S-L8 ended as `INCOMPLETE_SAFETY_STOP` with `PREREGISTERED_RULE_RELEASED_EXISTING_LEGITIMATE_NEGATIVE_CONTROL`.

## Final product decision

- Core correctness: **PASS**.
- Safety precision: **PASS** with conservative human REVIEW routing.
- Extraction robustness: **PASS**, including deterministic pre-call partitioning.
- Full automation generalization: **NOT ACCEPTED**.
- Known limitation: human-review burden can exceed 30% on complex clean PDFs.
- Production remains unchanged and no Production write authority is implied.
- S-M is not authorized and was not started. The previous prospective S-M contract is superseded; there is no current S-M contract.

No additional Phase 3E.2 bounded semantic repair is authorized. Reducing the remaining atomicity review false positives requires a materially stronger representation or reviewer, such as a governed second-stage or local-model reviewer, richer Proposition IR, learned semantic equivalence under explicit safety controls, or a dedicated human-review workflow. These are deferred architecture directions, not Phase 3E.2 work.
